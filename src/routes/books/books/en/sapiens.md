## Notes

The argument that stayed with me is that the things holding a large group of
people together — money, nations, companies — only work because everyone agrees
to act as if they are real. That is a strange and useful thing to notice at
sixteen.

## Where I pushed back

It moves very fast, and the speed hides the disagreements. Whole debates among
historians get one confident sentence. I liked the book more when I started
treating it as a very good set of questions rather than a set of answers.
