## Notes

Affordances, signifiers, feedback, mapping. Four words that made me
insufferable for about a month, because suddenly every microwave in the house
was worth complaining about.

The useful part for me was the argument that blaming the user is almost always
the lazy answer. I have caught myself writing an error message that blamed the
person reading it more than once since.

## Applied

Redid the settings screen on one of my projects after this. Fewer options,
clearer defaults, and the destructive action stopped sitting next to the safe
one.
