## Notes

Most programming books teach you a language. This one teaches you the habits
you are supposed to have picked up on your own and never did.

The chapter on orthogonality is the one I would hand to past me. Every project
I abandoned, I abandoned because changing one thing meant changing nine.

## Lines I wrote down

- Don't live with broken windows.
- Make it easy to reuse, or people will not reuse it.
- Your knowledge has a half-life. Keep investing.

## After

I started keeping an engineering notebook because of this book, and I have kept
it for four months, which is longer than any habit I have started from a
YouTube video.
